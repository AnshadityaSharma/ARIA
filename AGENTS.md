# ARIA Repository Instructions

Before planning, implementing, reviewing, or testing ARIA, read `context.md` in full and treat it as the project's source of truth.

Keep the architecture capability-driven rather than application-driven: discover applications, files, windows, websites, and other targets dynamically whenever possible. Define reusable capabilities instead of enumerating target-specific behavior.

Follow the phases and development rules in `context.md`. Do not introduce later-phase systems or heavyweight dependencies before the current phase is reliable and tested.
